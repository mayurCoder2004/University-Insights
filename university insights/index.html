Eligibility Criteria

Basic requirements to study MBBS abroad

Basic Requirements

Minimum 17 years of age

50% in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) in 12th

NEET Qualification

Required Documents

10th & 12th Mark Sheets

Valid Passport

NEET Score Card

Birth Certificate

Important Notes

All documents must be submitted with English translation if in regional language

Passport should have minimum 18 months validity

Medical fitness certificate may be required by some universities
